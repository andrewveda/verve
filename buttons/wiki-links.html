What you're practising

Every click is a small round of creative-critical thinking.

Diverge. Before you click, open up. Physics could lead to Einstein, to energy, to war, to music. List the doors, including the unlikely ones. Your knowledge expands with every article you open. On the way to connecting two very different topics, you learn about the places in between, so you finish knowing far more than when you started.

Converge. Then narrow down. Which door gets you closer to the goal? Judge your options, pick one and commit.

Connect. The best routes join two ideas that seem unrelated. Your path is a record of how you made them touch.

Reflect. Where did you widen your thinking? What new knowledge have you gained?
