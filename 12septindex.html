Verve
/vɜːrv/
noun
  1. Vigour and spirit; enthusiasm, especially as expressed in art, speech, or performance.
  2. Energy and liveliness of style or manner.
French, from Old French verve — "caprice, style" — ultimately from Latin verba, "words."
