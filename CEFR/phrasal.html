A phrasal verb combines a base verb with a preposition or adverb. The result is a meaning you usually cannot guess from the individual parts. Give means to hand over. Give up means to quit.

Why it matters

They are the pulse of conversational English. While textbooks teach you to "tolerate" or "discover," native speakers will always put up with or find out.

The habit

Sounding natural

Ditch the formalities. Embrace the pair.

To sound fluent, swap stiff single verbs for their dynamic, two-part equivalents:

  • Please extinguish the fire.Please put out the fire.
  • I will postpone the meeting.I will put off the meeting.
  • She rejected his offer.She turned down his offer.
  • Let us examine the data.Let us look over the data.
